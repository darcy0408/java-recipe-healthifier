Chicken Parmesan
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp soybean oil
- 1/4 cup butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 8 oz spaghetti
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese
- 4 chicken breasts

Instructions:
1. Coat the chicken in whole wheat flour and panko breadcrumbs.
2. Heat the soybean oil and butter in a skillet.
3. Stir the soy sauce and brown sugar together.
4. Serve the chicken over spaghetti with parmesan cheese and mozzarella cheese.
